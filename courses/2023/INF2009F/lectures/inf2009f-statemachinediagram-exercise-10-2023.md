**Exercise Task --- State Machine Diagram**

Draw a state machine diagram that will depict the object states that an object of a Circle class can be in. These statuses are important for reporting, decision making and to easily identify those circles that are highly successful and whose progress has been exceptional.

**Additional information:**

*   When a request for a Circle is received the Khwela Womxn Team (KWT) will need to approve the request (Once the request is approved, they will allocate a moderator to manage and run the Circle).
*   A Circle is active when it has Circle members on board.
*   The KWT decided that circles will be assigned a bronze, silver or gold status depending on the number of members in the Circle (bronze ≡ 5 or more members, but less than 10; silver ≡ 10 or more, but less than 15; gold ≡ 15 or more but less than 20).
*   The KWT also wants to incentivise the circles by giving them a badge for having an impact or for attendance record that are exceptional. These badges will be assessed every 6 months to decide whether the Circle will maintain their status or go to a nobadge state.
*   However, if the circles have been inactive for more than a month they will be tagged for investigation and facilitation.
*   Finally, circles could be disbanded if no activity has been taken place for more than 1 month.

Provide a brief description of the state machine diagram.
