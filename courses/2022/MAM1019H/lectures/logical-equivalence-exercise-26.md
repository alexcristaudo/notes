1.  P∧(Q∨∼Q)and(∼P)⇒(Q∧∼Q)

Q ∨ ~Q is always True

∴ P ∧ (Q ∨ ~Q) = P

Q ∧ ~Q is always False

By the contrapositive law:

(~P) ⇒ (Q∧~Q) = ~(Q∧~Q) ⇒ ~(~P)

~(Q ∧ ~Q) is always positive

~(Q ∧ ~Q) ⇒ P is true when P is true

Hence ~(Q ∧ ~Q) ⇒ P = P

∴ P∧(Q∨∼Q) and (∼P)⇒(Q∧∼Q) are logically equivalent
