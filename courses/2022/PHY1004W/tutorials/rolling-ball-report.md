## Method:

The piece of paper was laid on the floor, under and in front of the slope and was then secured with tape. A metallic object on the piece of string was hung from the slope (this was done by hanging the string over the slope and letting it come to rest hanging over the slope) and its location on the piece of paper was marked. The ball was then launched once from the slope to get an idea of where the ball will land, from the middle of the slope and as a result, the blue carbon sheet on top of the paper was placed, with the middle of the sheet placed roughly where the ball landed, and the sides of the sheet were placed such that they were parallel with the balls horizontal path. Tape was then used to secure the carbon sheet. Some spot on the slope was marked with a pen and the vertical distance to this spot was measured and recorded as _h_ on a laptop and a piece of paper. The ball was launched from that spot, with the center of the ball in line with this line (so that the ball is launched from the same position every time) for a total of 5 times. The launching steps was repeated 6 times to get 6 different heights. The carbon sheet was taken off and each spot on the piece of paper was measured and recorded (with its cluster, so the short cluster is marked with the short height). The length measured is the horizontal distance from the center of the blue spot to the spot that the ball is launched from (this is already marked on the sheet of paper)
